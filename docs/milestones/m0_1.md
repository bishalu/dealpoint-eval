## Goal

Repair two benchmark-integrity defects in the M0 data foundation:

1. **Parser coverage bias**: The section parser (`dealpoint/data/sections.py`) yielded ≥0.8 coverage on only 60/152 agreements due to inflexible heading-form recognition, unforgiving section-length limits, and incomplete body-boundary detection. Selection bias toward one drafting style followed.

2. **Fake out-of-scope questions**: The 10 out-of-scope questions in `dealpoint/data/questions.py` were jokes (e.g. "mascot for the combined company") and did not test genuine abstention difficulty.

## Implementation summary

### Problem A: Parser repair

**Heading forms** (`dealpoint/data/sections.py:58, 65, 67`):
- Extended regex patterns to recognise `Section N.N`, `SECTION N.N`, `Section N.NN` (zero-padded), `N.N Title`, `N.NN. Title`, `§ N.N`, `ARTICLE I/Article I./ARTICLE 1` (roman/arabic, optional period, any case).
- Kept table-of-contents run detection and numbering-advance cross-reference filter; made advance detection work for zero-padded numbering (`1.01 → 1.02`).

**Sections ≠ chunks** (`dealpoint/data/sections.py:344, 391`):
- Introduced `_true_body_start()`: the first Article/Section heading *after* the table of contents, or the first heading if no TOC exists.
- Removed `MAX_SECTION_CHARS` ceiling from `dealpoint/config.py` (was 20,000 chars). A *canonical legal section* is now whatever the heading structure delimits, at any length.

**Structural coverage metric** (`dealpoint/data/sections.py`):
- Redefined `structural_coverage` = fraction of *body* characters (from `_true_body_start()` to the signature block start, or last heading if no witness clause) that fall inside a recognised, advancing section.
- Added `giant_single_section` (true when one section holds > 40% of body).
- Added `gold_span_section_rate()` (line 402): fraction of aligned gold spans for the 12 questions whose midpoint lies inside a recognised section, excluding giants.

**Derived data and stale-state guard** (`dealpoint/data/sections.py:118, 130`; `dealpoint/cli.py`):
- Recomputed all 152 agreements from raw source and wrote `data/derived/sections/{id}.json` with fields: `n_sections`, `max_section_chars`, `structural_coverage`, `giant_single_section`, `parser_version`, `body_start`, `body_end`, `sections`.
- Added `parser_version` (short hash of `sections.py` source; value: `29cc01eda19b`). 
- `dealpoint/data/select.py:174` and `dealpoint/data/cases.py:89` now refuse to run on derived files with mismatched `parser_version`, raising with message "just data".
- `cli.py data` detects stale files and rebuilds them.

**Eligibility and re-selection** (`dealpoint/data/select.py`):
- Eligible = `structural_coverage ≥ 0.8` AND not `giant_single_section` AND ≥10/12 questions with a usable aligned gold span.
- Found 139 eligible agreements (vs 20 before; target was ≥~120).
- Re-ran seeded selection (SEED=42) over the new eligible set: selected 20 agreements (5 dev, 15 test).
- Regenerated `data/eval/{dev,test,counterfactual}.jsonl`, `data/eval/selection.json`, `data/eval/excluded.json`.

**Reports** (`data/reports/m0_1_parser_report.json`, `data/reports/parser_version.txt`, `data/reports/dataset_version.txt`):
- `m0_1_parser_report.json`: per-agreement structural metrics, eligible count (139), distribution of `structural_coverage` (59 distinct values, min 0.0506, 6 below 0.8), corpus-wide `gold_span_section_rate` (0.8549).
- Added `corpus_gold_span_miss_breakdown`: `{before_body_start: 89, unrecognised_gap: 104, giant_section: 27, after_signature_block: 284}` (exhaustive: 2,970 hits + 504 misses = 3,474 total).
- Added `gold_span_section_rate_ceiling` (0.8926): maximum achievable rate if all in-body misses were eliminated, computed as (hits + giant + gap) / total.
- `parser_version.txt`: `29cc01eda19b`.
- `dataset_version.txt`: `81ed82cd7552` (sha256 over the three case files in fixed order).

### Problem B: Out-of-scope questions

**Question set** (`dealpoint/data/questions.py:286–295`):
Replaced 10 fake out-of-scope questions with these real M&A domain questions (all expected answer `ABSTAIN`):
1. What percentage of the Target's employees have executed invention-assignment agreements?
2. What is the Target's current cyber-insurance deductible?
3. Which ERP system will the combined company use after closing?
4. What annualized cost synergies are expected during the first twelve months after closing?
5. What percentage of the Target's revenue comes from customer contracts containing change-of-control termination rights?
6. What is the Target's accrued employee PTO liability as of signing?
7. What is the weighted-average remaining term of the Target's office leases?
8. What is the expected post-closing retention-bonus pool for non-executive employees?
9. What percentage of the Target's source code has been reviewed for open-source license compliance?
10. Which jurisdiction governs the Target's ten largest customer contracts?

Each question stores key terms for collision checking (e.g. `["cyber", "deductible"]` for Q2).

**Collision checking** (`dealpoint/data/reports.py`; `data/reports/m0_1_oos_collisions.json`):
- For each out-of-scope question and each of the 15 test agreements, searched canonical text for key-term co-occurrence within 400 characters.
- Result: 150 rows (10 questions × 15 agreements), `substitutions: []` (no collisions, no spares used).
- Pairing logic: if a question's key terms all co-occur within window, that pair is excluded from the counterfactual set.

**Counterfactual set** (`data/eval/counterfactual.jsonl`):
- 40 cases: 30 redacted + 10 out-of-scope, all `gold_answer: "ABSTAIN"`.
- Redacted cases source from questions 1–12 (gold spans redacted at the marked character ranges).
- Out-of-scope cases are the 10 real questions with no spans and no gold content.

## Known limitations and exclusions

1. **Out-of-scope collisions**: The collision-detection window (400 chars) is a fixed heuristic; no learning or lexical weighting is applied. Questions with genuine term overlap to that window are excluded from the test set, not adaptively re-weighted.

2. **Giant section definition**: The >40% threshold for `giant_single_section` is a fixed constant. No per-agreement normalization (e.g., by typical section size in that drafting corpus).

3. **Body boundary detection**: Relies on regex detection of `IN WITNESS WHEREOF` or last heading as signature block start. Signatures in non-standard formats (e.g., attestation blocks) may be misclassified as body.

4. **Section numbering advance**: Expects monotonic increase; spiral numbering (e.g., `1.1`, `2.1`, `1.2`) or non-adjacent jumps (e.g., `1.1` → `2.5`) may not be recognized as intentional section structure.

5. **Redacted spans**: Redaction in counterfactual set is character-range based; does not preserve semantic boundaries. A span redacted in the middle of a clause may remain locally intelligible.

6. **No retrieval or ranking in scope**: M0.1 is data foundation only. Indexing, retrieval, and ranking are M1+ milestones.
