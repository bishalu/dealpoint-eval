# q06 — MAE Forward-Looking Standard
Where: the defined term "Material Adverse Effect" (or "Company MAE") — look it up directly.
Distinguishes: "would (reasonably) be expected to", bare "would", "could (reasonably) be expected to", other phrasing, or none.
Trap: the phrase is normally near the opening of the definition; do not confuse a carve-out qualifier further down with the main test.
